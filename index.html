Duo UI rulebook

Try it on your Duo: open this page with #device added to the end of the link. It fills the screen, switches between Front and Full as you fold, and is tappable: the orb button, the left handle, and the date line (switches Paper and OLED). Everything here is for the iPhone Duo only. The regular iPhone, iPad and PWA don't change. Below is the Dash with every rule so far applied, built from your real numbers and photos, then the rules themselves.

The rules so far

1Edge to edge

No cards inside cards. Lists, cubes, the sales summary and the promo run the full width of their half, with no side margins or rounded corners. Row lines start where the text starts.

2Content runs under the column

Content goes to the right edge and scrolls under Apple's column. Only the status pill and the action button sit on top. Buttons and arrows stay left of the column.

3Right column is for actions

One glass button per page. It always shows a real icon, never a plain "...": the page's main action, or on the Dash a glowing glass orb (Theo) with a faint ring that hints it opens. A tap stacks the actions up the column and fans their names out to the left.

4Left menu is for going places

A glass menu slides over the page from the left edge, on both screens, highlights the page you're on, and closes when you pick one. Closed, it's just a small handle.

5Two columns on the Full screen

List on the left, detail on the right, first item open automatically. The week grid and the Dash are the exceptions.

6Dash

To-do on the left; Theo's line on top of the left, above Quick Tasks. Widgets on the right in this order: promo, cubes, sales summary. The Dash button holds Ask Theo, Nudge and Edit layout. The store name floats in a glass bubble at the bottom left; tap it to switch locations.

7Cubes slide, no 3D

Swipe slides the next face in; automatic changes cross-dissolve, so nothing swings outside the edge.

8Two glass themes, no brand colour

Paper is shades of beige glass; OLED is shades of dark glass on black. Status colours are soft tinted glass, not hard tones: sage for done, rose for late. Photos stay photos.

9Liquid glass overlays

Menu, action stack, names and dialogs are see-through glass. Heavier frost wherever you type.

10Dialogs

Full screen: open as the right-half inspector. Front screen: an edge-to-edge sheet above the keyboard, never under the column.

11Page template and type

No page title; one quiet 15pt date line at the top (nothing on pages where the date doesn't matter). Section titles 13pt medium, muted, lined up with row text. Rows 17pt regular, counts 15pt on the right. Labels 13pt regular, numbers 28pt medium. Only the headline sales figure is big, and it's centered with its label above and chips below.

12Date picker lives in its block

Today, Yesterday, Week and Month are a small glass segmented row inside the sales block, under its label, not a bar across the top.
